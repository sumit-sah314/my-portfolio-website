About Me

I'm an Applied Mathematics and Computer Science student at Texas State University, interested in machine learning, AI, and data science. I love figuring out how things work, especially when math and code come together to solve real problems. I want to understand how AI learns from data, why it sometimes fails, and how to make it more reliable.

I've done research in adversarial machine learning, AI robustness, and AI web agents, and interned as a machine learning engineer at Microsoft. Outside of school, I build projects, try new tech, and share what I learn.
